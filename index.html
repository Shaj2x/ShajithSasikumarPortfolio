The real questions

What people actually ask before they buy.

Where does my customer data go?

Onto your own accounts, not mine. The system runs on infrastructure you own and can revoke. I will tell you exactly which services touch a caller's details before anything is built, and you approve that list.

What does it cost to set up?

Quoted per project after the first call. There is no honest number I can put on a page without knowing your call volume and what you want handled. What I will promise is a written scope with the price on it before you commit to anything.

Will it sound like a robot?

Not any more. The voices are good now, and that is not where these fail. They fail by staying calm and scripted through a moment that needed a person. An emergency, a complaint, someone upset. So before launch we agree what those moments are and it hands the call straight to you. That is the part most people selling this never mention.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

Every call is logged with a transcript, so you can see what happened rather than guess. Anything it is unsure about gets escalated to you with the context attached instead of being answered badly.

Do I own it?

Yes. It runs on your accounts, it is documented, and you can take it elsewhere. I am not a subscription you cannot leave.
